Who Checks on the Manager?

April 2026 · 2 minute read · First published on LinkedIn

Something I keep seeing across organisations. We say people do not leave jobs, they leave managers. True. But here is what nobody talks about: what happens to the manager?

The middle manager caught between leadership expectations and team needs. Responsible for everyone's engagement while nobody checks on theirs. Expected to motivate, develop and retain, often without being motivated, developed or retained themselves.

The most disengaged person in your organisation might be the manager everyone is relying on.

Here is what I have observed: people do not just disengage. They lose something specific.

Their spark. Their why. Their place. Their voice. Their people. Their pace. Their balance. Their trust.

Eight states. Eight root causes. Eight different interventions.

And managers go through these exact same states. Just quietly. While still showing up.

You can fake an engagement score. You cannot fake a state of mind. Fake it till you make it only lasts so long.

That is why I have been building a diagnostic tool. Not just for employees. For managers too. Because you cannot pour from an empty cup, and right now a lot of cups are empty.

Whether you manage people or not: what is your engagement score? And more importantly, what is the actual state of your mind?

Practitioner observation. The eight states are a working framework in development, not a validated instrument. First published on LinkedIn, April 2026.
